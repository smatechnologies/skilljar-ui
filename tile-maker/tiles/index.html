Make many at once

One tile per line: colour, label, title, subtitle, corner tag. Colour is blue, beige, sky or night; use | in the title for a line break; leave a part empty with nothing between the commas. Each tile downloads as its own file, named from its title. Your browser may ask once to allow several downloads.
